# Metric catalog

This catalog is generated from `config/metrics.yml` (schema `2.0.0`).

Rates use safe division and remain null when their denominator or required evidence is missing. Synthetic attendance and chronic-absence rate tests use an absolute rounding tolerance of `0.000001`; count and instructional-day comparisons must match exactly.

| Metric | Description | Formula | Grain | Allowed dimensions | Source model | Refresh expectation | Privacy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| enrolled_student_count | Distinct trusted student tokens represented in the reporting grain. | COUNT(DISTINCT student_key) | school year, district, school, and grade | school_year, district_key, school_key, grade_level | mart.mart_enrollment_trends | After each successful validated pipeline and dbt build. | sensitive_aggregate |
| possible_attendance_days | Count of trusted daily attendance opportunities represented in the grain. | COUNT(daily_attendance_key) | school year, district, school, and grade | school_year, district_key, school_key, grade_level | mart.mart_attendance_summary | After each successful validated attendance pipeline and dbt build. | sensitive_aggregate |
| attended_days | Attendance minutes expressed as equivalent full instructional days. | SUM(minutes_attended) / 420 | school year, district, school, and grade | school_year, district_key, school_key, grade_level | mart.mart_attendance_summary | After each successful validated attendance pipeline and dbt build. | sensitive_aggregate |
| attendance_rate | Share of possible instructional minutes attended. | SUM(minutes_attended) / NULLIF(SUM(possible_minutes), 0) | school year, district, school, and grade | school_year, district_key, school_key, grade_level | mart.mart_attendance_summary | After each successful validated attendance pipeline and dbt build. | sensitive_aggregate |
| chronically_absent_student_count | Students at or above the configured absence-rate threshold. | COUNT(student_key) FILTER (WHERE absence_rate >= chronic_absence_threshold) | school year, district, school, and grade | school_year, district_key, school_key, grade_level | mart.mart_chronic_absenteeism | After each successful validated attendance pipeline and dbt build. | sensitive_aggregate |
| chronic_absenteeism_rate | Share of students with measurable attendance at or above the configured threshold. | chronically_absent_student_count / NULLIF(enrolled_student_count, 0) | school year, district, school, and grade | school_year, district_key, school_key, grade_level | mart.mart_chronic_absenteeism | After each successful validated attendance pipeline and dbt build. | sensitive_aggregate |
| records_processed | Rows loaded by a pipeline run across its source files. | audit.pipeline_run.records_loaded when at least one source file was discovered | pipeline run | pipeline_name, pipeline_status | mart.mart_pipeline_health | After every pipeline completion. | internal |
| records_rejected | Rows rejected by contract or staging validation for a pipeline run. | audit.pipeline_run.records_rejected when at least one source file was discovered | pipeline run | pipeline_name, pipeline_status | mart.mart_pipeline_health | After every pipeline completion. | internal |
| blocking_error_count | Blocking quality failures recorded by the latest quality run. | audit.data_quality_rule_run.blocking_failure_count | pipeline run and latest data-quality run | pipeline_name, quality_status | mart.mart_data_quality_scorecard | After every data-quality validation. | internal |
| warning_count | Warning-severity failures recorded by the latest quality run. | SUM(rule failure_count) FILTER (WHERE severity = warning) | pipeline run and latest data-quality run | pipeline_name, quality_status | mart.mart_data_quality_scorecard | After every data-quality validation. | internal |
| data_quality_pass_rate | Share of enabled rules that passed in the latest quality run. | passed_rule_count / NULLIF(enabled_rule_count, 0) | pipeline run and latest data-quality run | pipeline_name, quality_status | mart.mart_data_quality_scorecard | After every data-quality validation. | internal |
| pipeline_success_rate | Binary run-level success indicator, aggregatable across pipeline runs. | 1 when pipeline_status = completed, otherwise 0 | pipeline run | pipeline_name, pipeline_status | mart.mart_pipeline_health | After every pipeline completion. | internal |
| source_freshness_hours | Hours since the most recently loaded source file for the pipeline run. | EXTRACT(EPOCH FROM (CURRENT_TIMESTAMP - MAX(source_file.loaded_at))) / 3600 | pipeline run | pipeline_name, pipeline_status | mart.mart_pipeline_health | Recomputed on every dbt build. | internal |
| reporting_readiness_score | Weighted score combining pipeline success, quality pass rate, and freshness. | 100 * (0.4 * pipeline_success_rate + 0.4 * data_quality_pass_rate + 0.2 * freshness_sla_pass) | pipeline run | pipeline_name, pipeline_status, quality_status | mart.mart_reporting_readiness | Recomputed on every dbt build after quality validation. | internal |
| assessment_event_count | Trusted assessment events represented in the approved aggregate result. | COUNT(assessment_key) | school year | school_year | mart.mart_reporting_readiness | After each successful validated assessment pipeline and dbt build. | sensitive_aggregate |
| assessment_proficiency_rate | Share of trusted assessment events meeting or exceeding the approved standard. | COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE is_proficient) / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) | school year | school_year | mart.mart_reporting_readiness | After each successful validated assessment pipeline and dbt build. | sensitive_aggregate |

The chronic-absence threshold is configured with the dbt variable `chronic_absence_threshold` and defaults to `0.10`. The reporting freshness SLA is configured with `source_freshness_sla_hours` and defaults to `24` hours.
